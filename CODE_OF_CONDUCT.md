# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Orbit to be a respectful, practical and welcoming project for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, direct and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for impact.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's data without permission.
- Make room for maintainers to prioritize safety, maintainability and user consent.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized attention or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information, credentials, screenshots, logs or recordings without explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions or ignoring maintainer boundaries.
- Encouraging unsafe desktop automation, credential theft or abuse.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions or block participants whose behavior harms the project community or user safety. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented when appropriate and focused on restoring a healthy working environment.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner profile or other published project contact. Do not include private personal data beyond what is necessary to understand the concern.
